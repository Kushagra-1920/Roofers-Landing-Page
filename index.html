Questions homeowners ask before they call.

What does a new roof cost, and can I pay monthly?

For a single-story home with architectural shingles, North Texas replacements often fall between $9,000 and $20,000, and your free inspection turns that range into one fixed price. Monthly plans are available with approval in minutes and no prepayment penalty, subject to credit approval.

Will my insurance cover it?

Many policies cover sudden damage such as hail or wind, and many exclude wear from age. We photograph the damage, write the report and meet your adjuster, but the decision belongs to your insurer and depends on your policy.

How do I know you are not a storm-chasing company?

We have worked from Dallas since 2004, and our office address is at the bottom of this page. Our license and insurance numbers are on every quote, and we will put you in touch with homeowners near you.

Do I pay anything before the work starts?

On most replacements, no: payment is due after the final walkthrough, once you approve the roof. The one exception is a large job with specially ordered materials, and that is written on your quote before you sign.
